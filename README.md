# Retail Sales Exploratory Analysis

Exploratory analysis of 1,000 retail transactions across Beauty, Clothing and Electronics. I looked at customer age, sales by category and gender, average prices and quantity sold. Customers were 18 to 64, with an average age of about 41. I didn't look at sales over time, even though the data has dates, so that's the obvious next step. The dataset isn't in this repo.
